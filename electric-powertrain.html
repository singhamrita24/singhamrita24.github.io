Electric Powertrain

Packaging and integration of high voltage ancillary systems for Formula SAE Electric vehicle, including battery management system, fusing, relays, and high voltage interlock loop.

HV Ancillary System (2024)

Managed a redesign of the entire HV ancillary system to improve packaging and integration of the battery management system, fusing, relays, and high voltage interlock loop. This included designing a new layout for the HV components, selecting appropriate connectors and wiring, and ensuring compliance with safety standards. My work enabled shrinking the volume of the HV ancillary system by 2.7X while maintaining functionality and safety, which improved the overall packaging of the vehicle and allowed for a lowered center of gravity. Specified and sourced components for the HV ancillary system, including fuses, relays, and connectors. Designed the layout of the HV ancillary system to optimize space, and manage creepage and clearance requirements. Designed jigs and fixtures to enable low-tolerance manufacturing of the HV ancillary system. Manufactured and assembled the HV ancillary system, including wiring. Validated the performance of the HV ancillary system through integration into the vehicle.

Electric Powertrain Integration (2025)
